OUR PURPOSE

Why does Bidesh Padhna exist?

Too many bright Nepali students give up on studying abroad because the process feels impossible. We built the platform we wished existed when we were students.

Eliminate confusion

The path from "I want to study abroad" to "I have my visa" has 40+ steps. We simplify this into an interactive, step-by-step roadmap.

Make it 100% free

Consultancy fees in Nepal can cost NPR 50,000–2,00,000. We offer superior AI guidance and support at zero cost.

Build community

Don't walk alone. Connect directly with other Nepali students already studying abroad and peer applicants going through the same phase.
